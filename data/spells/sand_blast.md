---
name: "Sand Blast"
level: 2
school: "Evocation"
casting_time: "1 action"
range: "30 feet"
components: "V, S, M (a pinch of desert sand)"
duration: "Instantaneous"
---

## Description
You hurl a spray of abrasive desert sand at a target. The creature must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw** or be blinded until the end of its next turn.

## At Higher Levels
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 2nd.
